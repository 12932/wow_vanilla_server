# Bug-hunt fixes: social services, mail and auctions (`social`)

Branch `fix/bughunt-social` (worktree `its worktree `), from master 0c5811f4. Tests in `src/game/tests_bughunt_social.rs` unless noted.

- The bidder list compares every auction against every id the client sends: **fixed** (5848500a; `a_long_outbid_list_is_answered_at_once`, 2.2 s to 50 ms).
- Built-in channel names with any suffix make channels that are never removed: **fixed** (323ddc2c; `an_empty_built_in_channel_goes`). An empty built-in channel goes like a custom one (mangos keeps them). Not done: per-player index for `Channels::of` and a 10-channel cap (bigger change, and a cap is a rule mangos doesn't have).
- Any player can read any mail's body by id: **fixed** (eac6de32; `a_mail_or_letter_is_read_only_by_its_reader`). A mail body goes only to its receiver (a returned mail's receiver is its sender). A letter's text goes only to someone holding an item with that `ITEM_FIELD_ITEM_TEXT_ID`. Anyone else gets an empty text. Books use `CMSG_PAGE_TEXT_QUERY`, which this doesn't touch.
- A cash-on-delivery payment that expires goes back to the buyer: **fixed** (4b8dfaf6; `an_expired_cod_payment_is_not_sent_back`). `return_to_sender` left alone: there the seller chooses to give the money back.
- The mail sweep's expiry acts on a mail it doesn't check again: **fixed** (cfa0d274; `a_mail_sent_back_since_the_sweep_found_it_is_kept`, through the new `mail::expire`).
- The group's roster at a logout shows the leaving member as online: **fixed** (ff30eaea; `the_roster_at_a_logout_shows_the_member_offline`).
- A player in a battleground who joins or forms a group has their battleground raid roster overwritten: **fixed** for the roster (3db43f87; `a_group_made_in_a_battleground_leaves_its_raid_roster_alone`). **Deferred**: leader-only commands using `home()` while in a bg raid. That changes which group a command acts on, so it is a design call.
- `join_all` checks "already queued" under one lock and queues under another: **fixed** (68cfe65f; `two_joins_at_once_queue_a_player_once`, races 4 threads 300 times).
- The owner list of a seller with many auctions is not sent at all: **fixed** (572688d3; `a_seller_with_many_auctions_is_sent_what_fits`). The owner and bidder lists now honour `list_from` and stop at 511 rows (the 32,766-byte cap), with the true total.
- The instance sweep tests every save against every online player: **fixed** (c117edca; `the_instance_sweep_looks_at_the_online_list_once`, 100 ms to 2 ms at 1,000 saves and 3,000 online).
- A friend's login or logout scans every online player's friend list: **fixed** (9caa49c5; `friends::tests::those_with_a_player_as_a_friend_follow_the_lists` and `a_login_storm_asks_each_login_once`).
- Every roster change does members-squared registry reads, and a map call per member: **deferred**. Reading online state once saves about 1,600 cheap reads per raid update. The real cost is the leader-flag map calls, and sending those only on a lead change or join/leave risks a member's flag going stale after a login or teleport. Needs care, not a quick fix.
- Party member stats are given for any player, to anyone: **fixed** (c4a34ff4; `a_members_stats_are_for_their_group`). Full stats go only to the player themselves, their group or their own group while in a bg raid. Anyone else is told offline.
- Money taken from mail past the cap is lost silently: **deferred**. It needs a choice: take what fits and leave the rest, or refuse. The 1.12 mail result has no gold-cap error, so a refusal would have to show some other error. User decides.
- Groups whose members are all gone for good are kept and swept forever: **deferred**. Checking "anyone online" costs what `next_leader` costs. The real fixes are an online-member count or expiring groups after N days, which is a policy choice.
- A failed bg-raid join leaves the player marked away from their own group: **fixed** (e38894e2; `a_full_raid_leaves_a_player_in_their_group`).
- A kick from one's own group while in a battleground sends `SMSG_GROUP_UNINVITE`: **deferred**. mangos-classic sends it unconditionally too, and the client effect is unverified (the review only inferred it). It needs a client-check handler for `SMSG_GROUP_UNINVITE` first.
- Stats kept for a player who just left their group: **fixed** (a940c4c4). The second lock keeps and sends stats only for players still grouped. No test: it is a race between two locks with no hook to hold it open. The stats and group suites pass.
- Dead code and misleading names in the auction paths: **fixed** (a0154503; rename only, no test). Module-doc "mangos-zero" wording left as is.
- Bids and cancels are taken on an auction that has expired but not been swept: **fixed** (30778125; `an_auction_run_out_takes_no_bid_nor_cancel`). No bid or buyout after expiry, and no cancel of an expired auction that has a bid. An unbid expired auction can still be cancelled: the item comes back the same way the sweep would return it, and `tests_p3_network_npc::an_auction_house_opens_sells_takes_bids_and_lists` (Thistle Tea parity) does exactly that.

Checks: `cargo clippy --all-targets` clean. `cargo nextest run --lib` 3415/3415. Extended run over the touched areas (mail, auction, group, bg, channel, instance, friend, guild, meeting): 547 tests, all passing except one flake, `arcane_missiles_channel_isnt_rolled_but_its_missiles_are`, which fails under load and passed 3 of 3 rerun alone. No packet layout changed, so the client check was not run.
