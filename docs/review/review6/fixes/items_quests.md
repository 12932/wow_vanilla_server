# Bug-hunt fixes: items, quests and loot

Branch `fix/bughunt-items_quests` (worktree `its worktree `), 12 commits on master 0c5811f4.

- A player can trade with themselves: **fixed**, 5ffa0340. `initiate` refuses `with == g` with Busy; `accept` returns early as a second guard. Test: `tests_bughunt_items::a_player_cant_trade_with_themselves`.
- The disenchant loot window does nothing for a real client: **fixed**, cdaf5ed6. `items_misc::handle` claims the loot packets only for an item with no map loot (`loot_state.item_loot`). Tests: `tests_bughunt_items::a_disenchants_window_takes_and_releases_through_the_protocol`; `tests_p10_player_items::a_disenchants_leftovers_go_into_the_bags_at_release` now takes through `CMSG_AUTOSTORE_LOOT_ITEM`.
- Lootable items can be opened from the buyback slots: **fixed**, ab5347dd. `open_as`, `wrap` (paper and target) and the open item window (`window`) refuse buyback slots. Tests: `tests_bughunt_items::a_clam_in_the_buyback_doesnt_open`, `nothing_in_the_buyback_is_wrapped`.
- Spells cast in the trade window count and spend reagents from the bank and the buyback: **fixed**, aab02394. `check` counts carried reagents only, totems use `tool_missing` the way a cast does, and `pay` takes carried items only. Test: `tests_rules::a_trade_enchant_wont_spend_banked_or_sold_reagents`.
- Skinning ignores untaken quest and free-for-all drops: **fixed**, 6b3b43ec. The new `Loot::looted_for_all` is `done` for a corpse's loot that fell to someone, else `is_empty`. `skinning_fails` and `skin` use it. Test: `tests_loot::a_corpse_with_an_untaken_free_for_all_drop_cant_be_skinned`.
- Quest-object activation every half second does a global groups lock and allocations per player per known node: **fixed in part**, 42617c7a. `wanted` (with its groups lock) is now worked out once per player per pass, only at the first chest that has a quest drop. A chest's loot is walked without building a `Vec`, so herbs and veins cost nothing. Not done: precomputing each table's quest items in `GameData`, and `giver_quests`' clones and events locks for quest-giver objects. Both are bigger changes for an unmeasured cost. No test: behaviour is unchanged, and the existing quest-chest tests cover it.
- A party-accept quest is offered at 30 yards but refused past 10: **fixed**, ffcbaf9a. `admit` takes `asked` (from `accept_asked`, the confirm path) and then checks no distance, as mangos does. Test: `tests_misc::an_escort_confirmed_twenty_yards_off_is_taken`.
- The bank's packet gate misses CMSG_OPEN_ITEM: **fixed**, 18cb9279. Test: `tests_bughunt_items::a_clam_in_the_bank_doesnt_open_away_from_a_banker`.
- A group's loot money gives no share to members on another map: **deferred**. The fix is a design call that the finding itself asks to have recorded in DECISIONS. One option pays absent sharers the way mangos does, which needs a cross-map money credit through the registry, `MapHandle::call` or mail. The other divides only among those present, which is not mangos's rule. The user should choose.
- Quest credit still goes to failed quests: **fixed**, c35bf303. Test: `tests_bughunt_items::a_failed_quest_gets_no_kill_credit`.
- The pockets and picked sets leak for creatures that despawn without dying: **fixed**, 960c5c86. The new `loot::forget_pockets` runs at death, in `take_creature` (every removal) and in `ai::respawn`. It also fixes a respawned creature that a script had despawned still counting as picked. Test: `tests_bughunt_items::a_creatures_pockets_go_as_it_leaves`.
- Picking a creature's pockets while another rogue has them open overwrites that rogue's loot: **deferred**. The fix is a behaviour choice. mangos (`EffectPickPocket`) shows the second rogue the same, not fully taken loot, but wvs keeps pockets per rogue (`pockets`, `picked_before`). The options are to refuse the second rogue while the window is open, or to share it the way mangos does. Each changes what a player sees.
- A tradable item's attach failure is silently ignored at trade completion: **fixed**, 8324328b. On `Err` the item is logged at `warn` and mailed to its new owner with `inventory::mail_back`. No test: the review found no case today where the plan and `attach_item` disagree.
- MAX_MONEY is declared three times: **fixed**, d878c90d. Trade and vendor now use `inventory::MAX_MONEY`.
- The tests reach loot through direct calls that hide routing bugs: **fixed in part**, cdaf5ed6. The p10 disenchant test now goes through the protocol, and a new protocol test covers take plus one release. The direct `loot::take_item` calls in `tests_p3_items_professions.rs:443` and `tests_parity_misc.rs:1228` are left as they are, since the new test covers the routing.

Checks: `cargo clippy --all-targets` clean; fast suite 3410/3410; extended tests on loot, skinning, trade, quests, bank, vendors, chests and escorts 78/78. No packet layout changed, so no client check.
