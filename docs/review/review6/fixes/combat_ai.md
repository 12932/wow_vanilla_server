# Bug-hunt fixes: combat, creatures and pets (`combat_ai`)

Branch `fix/bughunt-combat_ai` (worktree `its worktree `), 14 commits on master `0c5811f4`. Clippy is clean, the fast suite passes (3,413), and `cargo extended` passes with `WVS_DATA` (4,179). No packet layout or create changed, so the client check wasn't run.

- **A player who possesses a creature can make it cast any spell in the game**: **fixed**, `b5539099`. Test: `tests_pets2::a_possessed_unit_casts_no_spell_it_doesnt_have`. `tests_p8_movement_inbound::a_pets_stop_and_a_possessed_casters_ground_spell_go_as_its_master_says` relied on the bypass, so it now gives the wolf its spell.
- **A possessed unit's movement is taken unchecked** (also map_core's `possess.rs` finding): **fixed**, `9f36b711`. Players and creatures now share one guard API in `move_guard` (`Mover`, `held_back_of`, `stripped_of`, `too_fast_at`, `launched_with`). A possessed player is checked with their own guard. A possessed creature has a guard in `PetState`. Root orders count from the controller's client, a launch counts, and a GM controller is exempt. Tests: `tests_pets2::a_possessed_player_moves_no_faster_than_they_can_nor_rooted`, `tests_pets2::the_eye_of_kilrogg_goes_no_faster_than_it_can`.
- **A possessed or charmed creature that goes without dying leaves its master's client stuck on it**: **fixed**, `56212b42` (`pets::leaving`, called from `Map::remove_creature`). Test: `tests_pets2::a_possessed_creature_removed_gives_its_master_their_own_back`.
- **The tap is lost when the tapper dies, feigns death, vanishes or leaves**: **fixed**, `6cc92a6e`. mangos-classic and Thistle Tea agree on this. Test: `tests_p6_misc_rewards::a_tap_outlasts_its_tapper_leaving_the_fight`.
- **A creature is tapped by whoever it aggroes, not by who damages it**: **deferred**, as a call for the user. Thistle Tea taps on combat entry (`Mob.combat_entered` → `maybe_tap`, which an aggro reaches too), so wvs follows Thistle Tea. mangos-classic taps only in `DealDamage`. Nothing is in DECISIONS yet.
- **A creature that evades without walking never "reaches home"**: **fixed**, `3849237c` (`home_reached` helper). Test: `tests_p3_ai_events::a_sessile_creature_that_evades_is_home_at_once`.
- **A creature left with only an unseen foe on its list stands in combat for good**: **fixed**, `a6c72388`. Only a passive creature can reach this state: for a fighting one, `forget` → `turn_to_next` already evades. Test: `tests_p6_ai_melee::a_passive_creature_left_with_only_an_unseen_foe_goes_home`.
- **Every creature death scans all 66,000 spawns**: **fixed**, `cdae7e79` (`GameData::creature_spawn` and `creature_spawn_in`, backed by a `OnceLock` index). Test: `map::ai::tests::a_spawn_is_found_by_its_guid_and_its_respawn_time_is_its_own`.
- **A charmed creature turns on its master as the master leaves the map**: **fixed**, `f0560393`. Test: `tests_pets2::a_charmed_creature_let_go_as_its_master_leaves_turns_on_no_one`.
- **Each change of victim from a forget calls for help again**: **fixed**, `05ac6861` (mangos's `m_AlreadyCallAssistance`). Test: `tests_p3_combat3_assist::a_creature_turning_to_its_next_foe_calls_no_one_again`.
- **A player's pet is noticed from as far as a player**: **fixed**, `341b6766`. This follows mangos-classic. Thistle Tea has no 0.65 factor. Test: `tests_pets2::a_pet_is_noticed_from_nearer_than_its_master`. Two reach tests now expect 65%.
- **A pet out of combat scans every awake creature at each combat check**: **fixed**, `df7aa9dc` (`Map::awake_any`, which stops at the first match). The existing pet-fight tests cover it.
- **The leash's timeout parses its config strings on the hot path**: **fixed**, `558b4317` (`RealmConfig::leash_brackets`, parsed once per config; a clone starts empty). Test: `tests_p7_combat_leash::the_leash_brackets_are_parsed_once_a_config_and_a_changed_copy_its_own`.
- **Overkill damage counts in the players' and NPCs' shares of a kill**: **deferred**, as a call for the user. mangos-classic and Thistle Tea (`DamageOrigin.record` of the unclamped `remaining`) both count the unclamped damage, so clamping it would change the rule beyond both.
- **A possessed player's master can cast the victim's whole spellbook**: **not a bug** by mangos-classic. `HandlePetCastSpellOpcode` checks `petUnit->HasSpell`, and for a player that is their spellbook. The real client's possess bar can't send it. Refusing it would go beyond mangos, which is a call for the user.
- **A creature removed without dying leaves its borrowed threat behind**: **fixed**, `e6465980`. `threat_sync::gone` runs in `take_creature`. `wiped` runs at script command 73 and Follow, and `removed` at a duel's pet cleanup. Test: `tests_p6_ai_melee::a_creature_removed_takes_the_threat_held_on_it`.
- **`die_rewarding`'s respawn time is written as a puzzle**: **fixed**, `8d96c768` (a refactor with no change in behaviour).
